# Skill: Exa

Use for fresh public web search and research.

## Prefer it when
- Need current news, recent releases, companies, people, research or public facts.
- Need external information that may have changed recently.

## Avoid when
- The answer should come from a local project, private account, n8n or another connected source.

## Pair with
Firecrawl when deeper page extraction or multi-page research is needed.
